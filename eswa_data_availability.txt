Data Availability Statement

The data used in this study come from a New York City coffee shop chain. Raw transaction-level records cannot be shared because of commercial confidentiality. Aggregated daily-level statistics sufficient to reproduce the main experimental results are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.

Source code for model training, evaluation, and figure generation is publicly available at:

https://github.com/qzpthuhhu/eswa-retail-forecasting
